# Clean Energy Initiative Roadmap

## Phase 1: Grid Stabilization
- Retrofit existing Stark Tower distribution stack.
- Deploy predictive load balancing with AI-assisted controls.

## Phase 2: Municipal Pilots
- Partner with 3 coastal cities for reactor-backed microgrids.
- Publish transparent safety metrics and downtime reports.

## Phase 3: Global Expansion
- License simplified reactor modules for humanitarian corridors.
- Train regional engineering teams and emergency response crews.
