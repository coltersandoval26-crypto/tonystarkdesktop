# Arc Reactor v4 - Design Notes

## Objectives
- Improve sustained output while reducing thermal spikes.
- Increase resilience under EMP and rapid altitude changes.
- Simplify maintenance access for in-field repairs.

## Materials Under Evaluation
- Palladium-alternative lattice composites.
- Gold-titanium microchannel cooling mesh.
- Ceramic-insulated magnetic confinement ring.

## Risks
- Heat sink saturation during high-thrust multi-vector maneuvers.
- Coupling instability when using experimental boost modules.
- Rare but critical harmonics under damaged-state operation.
